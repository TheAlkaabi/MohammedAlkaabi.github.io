    About me

    I'm a Grade 12 student in the Advanced Science Program at Applied Technology Schools in Al Ain, with a 4.0 GPA and one clear goal: to study Computer Science in the United States.

    I like understanding how things work from the inside. That curiosity led me to build, on my own, a reverse-engineering tool that turns raw offsets into readable names; to apply a trained YOLO model to track falcons; and to work on a hydraulic glove for hand rehabilitation.

    I have also taken part in academic and innovation programs, founded a Mental Math Club, and contributed to builder.ae, an initiative to make AI education more accessible. I learn independently, break hard problems into parts, and enjoy turning ideas into working projects.
